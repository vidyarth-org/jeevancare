FAQs

Who is eligible for Jeevan Care services?
Individuals up to 35 years of age can avail of Jeevan Care services. There is no income restriction, and our initiative is open to everyone seeking quality healthcare support.
Is there any registration fee?
No. Registration with Jeevan Care is completely free of cost. We do not charge any fees for registration or assistance. Beware of any individual or organization claiming to register you for a fee—they are fraudulent.
Where will patients be admitted?
Jeevan Care facilitates admissions only in leading private hospital chains through their CSR programs.
What kind of healthcare support is provided?
We provide comprehensive support including hospital admission, essential medical care, preventive guidance, and long-term health advice to ensure well-being.
How can one register for Jeevan Care?
Registration can be completed at any JC authorised centres, online or through our official support channels at no cost. Submitting application form at centre is mandatory.

Who We Are ?

We are committed to providing youngsters, up to 35 years of age, a high-quality healthcare services. Our program offers free registration and completely free medical treatment, enabling admissions in top private hospital chains through CSR partnerships.

We go beyond immediate treatment by providing ongoing guidance, preventive care, and compassionate support, ensuring health management for individuals and families. We strive to build a society where quality healthcare is accessible to everyone, without financial or social barriers.

Our Vision
To create a society where quality healthcare is accessible, compassionate, and trusted by all.

Our Mission
To provide free, ethical, and comprehensive healthcare services for youngsters, supporting them with care, guidance, and dignity.
